# CODE CATALYST

**CODE CATALYST** is a sci-fi robot survival game where **code is your greatest weapon** and **survival is your only mission**.

Fight through a world overrun by rogue machines. As a lone engineer trapped in a collapsed research facility, you must outsmart hostile robots, scavenge resources, and battle players online to escape — or dominate.

---

## Features

### 🤖 Robot Enemy AI
- Enemies patrol, detect, and react dynamically to the player.
- Rogue robots adapt their attack patterns as the game progresses.
- Enemies can coordinate, flank, and overwhelm unprepared players.
- Boss-tier machines require tactical thinking to defeat.

### 🎮 Single Player Campaign
- Experience a full single-player survival story mode.
- Navigate through a collapsed sci-fi research facility.
- Unlock upgrades, discover secrets, and fight to escape.
- Progressive difficulty with increasingly dangerous robot waves.

### ⚔️ Multiplayer & Online Battles
- Jump into real-time online battles against other players.
- Compete in survival arenas — last engineer standing wins.
- Team modes: co-op survival against waves of rogue robots.
- Live matchmaking and online leaderboards.

### 🛡️ Sci-Fi Survival Mechanics
- Scavenge parts from defeated robots to craft upgrades.
- Manage resources: power cells, ammo, and repair kits.
- Environmental hazards — electric traps, laser grids, and collapsing structures.
- Your decisions shape the difficulty of each encounter.

### 🔫 Combat System
- Fast-paced, responsive gunplay and melee combat.
- Multiple weapon types: energy rifles, EMP grenades, plasma blasters.
- Dodge, roll, and use cover to survive firefights.
- Combo-based melee attacks to conserve ammo.

### 🌐 Online Leaderboards & Progression
- Earn XP and level up your engineer profile.
- Unlock cosmetic gear, weapon skins, and robot companion upgrades.
- Compete globally — climb the leaderboards in survival and PvP modes.
- Daily and weekly challenge missions for bonus rewards.

### 🏗️ Sci-Fi Environment
- Explore a vast collapsed research facility full of secrets.
- Dark corridors, neon-lit labs, server rooms, and reactor cores.
- Destructible environments and interactive objects.
- Dynamic lighting — use darkness and shadows to your advantage.

### 🔊 Immersive Audio
- Futuristic ambient soundscapes and tense music.
- Enemy audio cues — hear robots before you see them.
- Weapon sounds and environmental effects.
- Dynamic soundtrack that reacts to combat intensity.

### 💻 Windows Platform
- Native Windows executable — no installation required.
- Keyboard and mouse support for precise control.
- Optimised for a wide range of PC hardware.
- Designed for both casual and competitive play sessions.
